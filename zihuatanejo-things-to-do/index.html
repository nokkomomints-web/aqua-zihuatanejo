A short guide from La Madera Beach

Things to do in Zihuatanejo

Zihuatanejo is a fishing town on a sheltered bay on Mexico's Pacific coast, with the resort of Ixtapa just up the road.1 From La Madera you can walk to most of what's below. Walk and drive times are from Google Maps; the facts come from the sources at the bottom.

Beaches

Around the bay

Playa La Madera

Right below Aqua

Gentle waves, bay-view restaurants on the sand and a walkway along the beach.1

Playa La Ropa

33 min walk · 2.3 km

About 1.2 km of sand with little wave action, often called the best beach in the bay for swimming. From August to December, hotels there protect sea turtle nests and invite guests to sunset releases of the hatchlings.1

Playa Las Gatas

By boat from the pier · pier is a 9 min walk

Across the bay, reached by water taxi. A coral reef and an old stone breakwater shelter it from the open ocean, and it's the most popular snorkelling beach in Zihuatanejo.1

Playa Principal

5 min walk

The town beach, next to the fishing port. Boats come and go all day, so it's better for watching the harbour than for swimming.1

In town

The waterfront and the food

Paseo del Pescador

5 min walk

The tree-lined waterfront walk between the archaeology museum and the pier. Seafood restaurants, crafts, a small shell market, and lots of people out in the evening.1

Eat huachinango

The local dish

Whole red snapper, cooked a la veracruzana (with tomato sauce) or al mojo de ajo (with garlic).1

Museo Arqueológico de la Costa Grande

End of the town beach

Six rooms on the history, archaeology and culture of this stretch of the Guerrero coast.1

Mercado de Artesanías

Crafts market

Shell work, silver from Taxco, lacquerware from Olinalá and ceramics from Oaxaca.1

On the water

From the pier

Sport fishing

Pier is a 9 min walk

Fishing charters leave from the municipal pier. The area is known for sailfish, and the International Sailfish Tournament runs on the first weekend of May.1

Water taxi to Las Gatas

From the municipal pier

Pangas ferry people across the bay to Las Gatas from the docks.1

Day trips

A little further

Ixtapa

15 min drive

The planned resort next door: a hotel strip about 3 km long, golf courses, shops and nightlife.1

Isla Ixtapa

Small island off Ixtapa

Three beaches: Playa Varadero, Playa Cuachalalate and Playa Coral.3

Barra de Potosí

Fishing village on a lagoon, in Petatlán

Palapa restaurants with fresh fish on the lagoon and the beach, lagoon tours and kayaks, and whale-watching trips in winter. More than 200 bird species have been recorded there.4

Getting here

By air or by road

  • Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo International Airport (ZIH)15 min driveFlights from Mexico City and from the US and Canada, some of them seasonal.1
  • By road from Mexico CityAbout 6 hoursUsing the Autopista Siglo XXI through Morelia.1
