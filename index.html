The residence

Three private bungalows, on the sand.

Aqua sits directly on La Madera Beach. Restaurants line the shore, the boardwalk to the pier is a short walk, and water taxis leave the dock for Las Gatas. Each bungalow has its own entrance, air conditioning and a view of the water.

The location

On La Madera, a walk from the pier.

Zihuatanejo sits on a sheltered bay on Mexico's Pacific coast. La Madera is the beach just east of the town centre: swimmable water, restaurants along the sand, and the boardwalk that runs around to the pier and the water taxis.
