Aqua Residence · Zihuatanejo, Mexico

Beachfront bungalows on La Madera Beach

Aqua Residence is three private bungalows right on La Madera Beach in Zihuatanejo, on Mexico's Pacific coast. The beach is just down the stairs. Restaurants line the sand, and the walkway along the water takes you to the pier and the town centre on foot.

The beach

What La Madera is like

La Madera sits between the town beach and Playa La Ropa on Zihuatanejo's sheltered bay. The waves are gentle, there are bay-view restaurants along the sand, and a walkway runs the length of the beach.1 Guests at Aqua mention the bike path next to the property and walking to the centre in about 8 minutes.2
